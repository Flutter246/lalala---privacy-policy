Privacy Policy for Lalala

Effective Date: 02 May 2025

Welcome to Lalala ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services (collectively, the “Platform”), similar in function and operation to Airbnb.

We collect the following types of information:

a. Information You Provide

Profile Details: Profile picture, gender, biography, location.

Payment Information: Banking or payment details (handled by a third-party processor).

Listings and Bookings: Property details, availability, pricing, booking requests, communications.

Identity Verification: Government ID, selfie/photo for verification purposes.

b. Automatically Collected Information
Device Info: IP address, device type, OS, browser.

Usage Data: App interactions, pages viewed, time spent.

Location Data: If enabled, GPS location.

c. Information from Third Parties
Login via Google, Apple, or Facebook.

Payment providers, background check services (if applicable).

We use your information to:

Provide, maintain, and improve the Lalala Platform.

Facilitate bookings and payments.

Verify your identity and prevent fraud.

Personalize your experience.

Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

3. Sharing Your Information
We may share your data with:

Other Users: Hosts and guests see each other's relevant information during booking.

Service Providers: Payment processors, customer service platforms, analytics tools.

Legal Authorities: If required by law or to protect rights, safety, or property.

Business Transfers: If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

We retain your personal data as long as necessary to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:
